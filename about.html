About JQuiz - Your Hiragana Practice Tool

JQuiz is a simple, effective, and fun interactive quiz designed to help you master your Japanese syllabary. Whether you're a beginner just starting your Japanese learning journey or looking for a quick way to refresh your memory, JQuiz provides a straightforward way to solidify your knowledge.

Learning Japanese starts with Hiragana, and consistent practice is key. Our quiz focuses on recognition and recall, offering immediate feedback to help you learn faster.

How to Play

  1. Select a Game Mode: Choose between `a → あ` (English Romanization to Hiragana) or `あ → a` (Hiragana to English Romanization).
  2. Answer the Task: A character (or romanization) will appear as the "Task." Select the correct corresponding answer from the grid of buttons.
  3. Track Your Progress: Your overall stats (correct/total attempts) are displayed to help you monitor your learning progress.
