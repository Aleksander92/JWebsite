Project status

JQuiz is actively evolving into a full Japanese learning platform with more scripts and lesson types.

About JQuiz

JQuiz is an educational project focused on helping beginners build a strong reading foundation in Japanese. We start with hiragana because it is the first essential step for vocabulary, grammar and sentence reading.

What you can do right now

  • Practice both directions: romaji → hiragana and hiragana → romaji.
  • Get immediate visual feedback on each answer.
  • Track your personal accuracy with clear statistics.
  • Use the quiz on desktop and mobile devices.

Learning philosophy

We keep the experience simple on purpose:

  • Short sessions reduce fatigue and improve consistency.
  • Frequent repetition strengthens long-term memory.
  • Minimal distractions keep focus on character recognition.

Roadmap

  • Katakana mode for loanwords and names.
  • Kana writing hints and stroke-order references.
  • Topic-based vocabulary drills with spaced repetition.
  • Progress profiles and achievement milestones.
